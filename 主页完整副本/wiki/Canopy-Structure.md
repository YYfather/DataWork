# Canopy Structure

## Overview

Canopy structure refers to the spatial arrangement of foliage and stems in a plant community. It is a key factor controlling light interception, photosynthesis, and microclimate.

## Key Parameters

### 1. Leaf Area Index (LAI)
- **Definition**: Total one-sided leaf area per unit ground area
- **Units**: m² m⁻² (dimensionless)
- **Typical ranges**:
  - Crops: 1-6
  - Forests: 3-10
  - Grasslands: 1-4

### 2. Canopy Cover
- **Definition**: Fraction of ground covered by vegetation
- **Measurement**: Hemispherical photography, satellite imagery
- **Applications**: Remote sensing, crop monitoring

### 3. Leaf Angle Distribution
- **Definition**: Distribution of leaf inclination angles
- **Types**:
  - **Planophile**: Horizontal leaves (most light interception)
  - **Erectophile**: Vertical leaves (light penetrates deeper)
  - **Spherical**: Random angles (most common)
- **Parameter**: Leaf Angle Distribution Parameter (1 = spherical)

### 4. Clumping Index
- **Definition**: Spatial aggregation of foliage
- **Range**: 0 (completely clumped) to 1 (random)
- **Effects**: Reduces effective LAI

## Measurement Methods

### Direct Methods
1. **Leaf collection**
   - Destructive harvesting
   - Time-consuming but accurate
   - Used for calibration

2. **Litter traps**
   - Collect fallen leaves
   - Estimate LAI from litter mass
   - Seasonal measurements

### Indirect Methods
1. **Optical methods** (SunScan, LAI-2200)
   - Light extinction through canopy
   - Non-destructive
   - Quick measurements

2. **Hemispherical photography**
   - Upward-looking fish-eye photos
   - Gap fraction analysis
   - Canopy architecture

3. **Remote sensing**
   - Satellite imagery (NDVI, EVI)
   - Airborne LiDAR
   - Large-scale estimates

## Applications

### Agriculture
- Crop yield prediction
- Irrigation scheduling
- Disease detection

### Ecology
- Forest structure analysis
- Biodiversity assessment
- Carbon cycling

### Climate Science
- Energy balance
- Evapotranspiration
- Carbon sequestration
