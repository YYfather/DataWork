# Sunfleck Dynamics Observation

**Date**: 2026-06-08  
**Project**: Field Trial  
**Personnel**:  

---

## Objective

Conduct high-frequency PAR measurements under forest canopy to characterize sunfleck temporal dynamics and quantify their contribution to understory PAR.

## Method

### Site
- Mixed broadleaf forest understory
- Measurement period: 10:00-14:00 (peak solar elevation)

### Equipment & Setup
- SunScan probe (64 channels)
- Sampling frequency: 1 Hz
- Continuous recording: 4 hours

## Results

### Sunfleck Statistics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total measurements | 14400 |
| Sunfleck events | 342 |
| Mean duration | 12.5 s |
| Longest sunfleck | 47 s |
| PAR contribution | 18.3% |

### Characteristics
- Sunfleck frequency peaks at solar noon
- Most sunflecks last 5-20 seconds
- PAR during sunflecks is 5-8× background diffuse light

## Discussion
- Sunflecks contribute ~18% of understory PAR, critical for understory photosynthesis
- High-frequency (≥1 Hz) measurements needed to capture sunfleck dynamics; low-frequency sampling underestimates
