# Diffuse Fraction Comparison

**Date**: 2026-04-05  
**Project**: Method Study  
**Personnel**:  

---

## Objective

Compare the Beam Fraction measured by the SunScan BFS sensor under different weather conditions (clear, partly cloudy, overcast) to verify sensor reliability.

## Method

### Equipment
- SunScan probe + BFS sensor
- Handheld weather station

### Procedure
1. Set up BFS sensor in open area with no obstructions
2. Measure continuously for 30 minutes under clear, partly cloudy, and overcast conditions
3. Record Beam Fraction every 30 seconds
4. Log weather conditions and solar elevation angle

## Results

### Beam Fraction Comparison

| Condition | Mean BF | Std Dev | Samples |
|-----------|---------|---------|---------|
| Clear | 0.85 | 0.03 | 60 |
| Partly Cloudy | 0.52 | 0.18 | 60 |
| Overcast | 0.12 | 0.05 | 60 |

### Analysis
- Clear sky BF is stable at 0.82-0.88, consistent with theoretical values
- Partly cloudy shows highest fluctuation (CV=35%), due to cloud movement
- Overcast BF is stable at low values, diffuse light dominates

## Conclusion
- BFS sensor performs reliably under stable weather conditions
- Under partly cloudy conditions, increase sampling frequency for representative means
- Recommend logging weather conditions during field measurements
